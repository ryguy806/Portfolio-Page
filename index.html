Backstory

In the ancient realm of Washington state, where a needle of steel pierces the clouds and streams of data flow alongside the mighty rivers, there emerged a champion of the digital arts. Back from his service aboard the great iron leviathans, Ryan Guelzo returned from beneath the crushing weight of the Endless Deep, his watch now ended, to begin a new journey as a humble apprentice, learning the arcane symbols of the web in the libraries of academia. There, steel and sonar gave way to logic and language. Through countless trials and tribulations-debugging sessions that lasted until dawn, battles against the dreaded legacy code dragons, and quests to optimize performance metrics—he honed his craft to perfection.

Now, as a Senior Frontend Developer, Ryan wields the powerful artifacts of modern web development with masterful precision. He has led parties of fellow developers through treacherous project dungeons, emerging victorious with pixel-perfect implementations and delighted clients. His reputation for transforming complex requirements into elegant, accessible user experiences has spread across the land. When not crafting enchanting interfaces, Ryan mentors junior adventurers, sharing the wisdom gained from seven years of questing in the ever-evolving landscape of web technologies.
