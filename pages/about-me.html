About Me

From nerd, to sailor, to developer, to Dad.

Real Backstory

I started my journey into Software Development like most. I played video games, and said to myself "Wouldn't it be cool to make games?!"

After graduating from Grandville High School, in Grandville, Michigan, my path to college led me to the military. Joining the Navy, I volunteered for submarine service. As a Fire-Control Technichian, I tracked contacts undetected and managed the Torpedo Fire-Control system onboard the USS Henry M. Jackson (SSBN 730).

Completing 5 years of service, I was once again a civilian. Taking an opportunity with a friend, I moved to Washington, a state that I had fallen in love with while stationed in Silverdale. After settling down and meeting my now wife at a board game night, I went back to school, turning my interest in games into earning a degree in Software Development from Green River College, while also starting my family.

It took a winding path and many hurdles to get to where I am. Looking forward to the next opportunity, I bring my passion for software development, a positive attitude, and a Bag of Holding, stuffed to the brim with skills, languages, and determination!
