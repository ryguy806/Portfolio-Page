About Me

From nerd, to sailor, to developer, to Dad.

Backstory

I started my journey into Software Development like most. I played video games, and said to myself, "Wouldn't it be cool to make games?!" Super Mario Bros set me off on a path I didn't know yet. I played every game I could get my hands on. Until I graduated.

After I graduated High School, I choose to go into the military. I walked into the recruiter's office for the Navy and started learning how to do things the "Navy way". I volunteered for Submarine service, becoming a Fire-Control Technichian (FT, now FTS). I tracked contacts undetected, and managed the Torpedo Fire-Control system.

I left the Navy after 5 years of service, and tried going to Grand Valley State University in Grand Rapids, Michigan. I ended up leaving before I could finish, but not ready to give up. I ended up moving out to Washington, where I worked a few stepping stone jobs in various customer service jobs. Meeting and helping people was something I enjoyed.

After a few years out here, I met my now wife Sam. She helped me go back to school and finally get my degree in Software Development. And right before I graduated, my first child was born. A couple years after that, my second.

I worked very hard to get where I am. I am a quick learner and an even better developer. I pick up new languages and frameworks so I can add more tools to my toolbox.
